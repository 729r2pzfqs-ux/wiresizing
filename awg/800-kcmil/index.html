Frequently Asked Questions

How many amps can 800 kcmil wire carry?

800 kcmil copper is rated 410 A at 60°C, 490 A at 75°C and 555 A at 90°C in conduit or cable (Table 310.16). Aluminum is rated 330, 395 and 445 A. Most terminations are 75°C-rated, so the 75°C figure is normally used.

What size conduit do I need for 800 kcmil wire?

Three 800 kcmil THHN conductors need at least the conduit size shown in the fill table above. Add the ground wire’s area for the actual installation.

What is the diameter of 800 kcmil wire?

The bare conductor is 800,000 circular mils (405.36 mm²). With THHN insulation the overall diameter is about 1.188 in and the area is 1.1085 in².
