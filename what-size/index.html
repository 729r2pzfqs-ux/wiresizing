Frequently Asked Questions

What wire gauge do I need for a 30-amp circuit?

A 30-amp circuit needs 10 AWG copper or 8 AWG aluminum under NEC Table 310.16. NEC 240.4(D) caps 10 AWG copper at 30A, so it is the minimum in either temperature column. Common uses include electric dryers, water heaters and small air conditioners.

What wire gauge do I need for a 50-amp circuit?

A 50-amp circuit needs 6 AWG copper or 4 AWG aluminum at 60°C (required for NM-B cable), or 8 AWG copper or 6 AWG aluminum with THHN and 75°C-rated terminations. Common uses: electric ranges, hot tubs, Level 2 EV chargers and sub-panels.

What gauge wire for a 100-amp sub-panel?

A 100-amp feeder or sub-panel needs 1 AWG copper or 1/0 aluminum at 60°C, or 3 AWG copper or 1 AWG aluminum with 75°C-rated terminations. A 100-amp dwelling service sized under NEC 310.12 can use 4 AWG copper or 2 AWG aluminum.
