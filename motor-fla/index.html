Motor Full-Load Amps (FLA) Tables

NEC Tables 430.247 through 430.250 list the full-load current used to size conductors, overload protection and short-circuit protection for motors. These are not nameplate values — they are the Code’s design figures for worst-case normal operation.

Single-Phase AC
Table 430.248 — 1/6 HP to 10 HP at 115/200/208/230 V
Three-Phase Induction
Table 430.250 — 1/2 HP to 500 HP at 200/208/230/460/575 V
DC Motors
Table 430.247 — 1/4 HP to 200 HP at 90/120/180/240/500/550 V
Two-Phase (4-Wire)
Table 430.249 — 1/2 HP to 200 HP at 115/230/460/575 V

How to Use Motor FLA Tables

The NEC uses these table values — not the motor nameplate — to size branch-circuit conductors (125% of the table FLA per 430.22), overload protection (based on actual nameplate), and short-circuit / ground-fault protection (varies by motor type per Table 430.52).

For multi-motor circuits, size the feeder for the sum of all motor FLAs plus 25% of the largest motor (430.24).
