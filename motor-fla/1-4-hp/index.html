Frequently Asked Questions

How many amps does a 1/4 HP motor draw?

The NEC tables list the full-load current for a 1/4 HP motor at each standard voltage and phase configuration. These are design values for sizing conductors and protection, not nameplate ratings. See the table above for all voltages.

What size wire do I need for a 1/4 HP motor?

Size the branch-circuit conductors at 125% of the table FLA (NEC 430.22). Look up the FLA for your voltage above, multiply by 1.25, then find that ampacity in the wire size tables.
