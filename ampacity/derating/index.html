Ampacity Derating Factors

Two independent derating factors reduce conductor ampacity: (1) ambient temperature above 30°C and (2) more than three current-carrying conductors in a raceway or cable. Apply both when both conditions exist.

NEC 310.15(C)(1). Applies when more than three current-carrying conductors are in a raceway, cable, or directly buried. Neutrals that carry only unbalanced current and equipment grounding conductors are not counted.

How to Apply Both Factors

When both conditions apply, multiply the table ampacity by both factors:

Derated ampacity = Table ampacity × temperature factor × bundle factor

Example: 12 AWG THHN (90°C column = 30 A) at 40°C ambient with 6 current-carrying conductors: 30 × 0.91 × 0.80 = 21.8 A
