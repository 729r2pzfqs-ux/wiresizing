Wiring Guides

Wire sizing rules change with the application: building wiring follows the NEC, boats follow ABYC E-11, and low-voltage DC and audio runs are usually limited by voltage drop long before ampacity. Each guide covers the governing standard, recommended gauges and practical tips.

Application Guides

Residential Home Wiring Guide

14 AWG copper for 15A lighting, 12 AWG for 20A outlets, 10 AWG for 30A dryers and water heaters, with NM-B limited to the 60°C column.

Automotive Wire Sizing Guide

Size 12V wire by current and the full round-trip length: a 20A load 10 feet from the battery needs 8 AWG to stay under 3% drop.

Solar Panel Wire Sizing Guide

PV conductors must carry 156% of module Isc (NEC 690.8); most residential strings use 10 AWG PV wire, upsized for long home runs.

Marine & Boat Wiring Guide

ABYC E-11 requires stranded copper and limits voltage drop to 3% for critical circuits and 10% for others; tinned boat cable is standard.

Speaker Wire Gauge Guide

Keep wire resistance under 5% of speaker impedance: for 8Ω, 16 AWG to about 50 feet, 14 AWG to 80 feet, 12 AWG to 125 feet.

EV Charger Wire Sizing Guide

EV chargers are continuous loads: a 40A charger needs a 50A circuit and a 48A charger a 60A circuit, sized at 125% (NEC 625.41).
