Metric Wire Sizes (IEC 60228)

Standard metric wire sizes used internationally. Defined by IEC 60228, these sizes are specified in mm² (square millimeters) cross-sectional area. Used in Europe, Asia, Australia, and most countries outside North America.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the metric equivalent of 12 AWG wire?

12 AWG (3.31 mm²) is closest to the IEC standard 2.5 mm² size, though 2.5 mm² is slightly smaller. The next metric size up is 4 mm², which exceeds 12 AWG. For an exact match, use the AWG to mm² conversion table.

What is 2.5 mm² wire used for?

2.5 mm² wire (roughly equivalent to 13 AWG) is the standard size for 20A outlet circuits in European and international wiring. It has an ampacity of approximately 20A and is used for kitchen, bathroom, and general-purpose circuits under IEC standards.

How does IEC 60228 differ from AWG?

IEC 60228 specifies wire sizes in mm² (cross-sectional area), while AWG uses an arbitrary gauge number where higher numbers mean smaller wire. IEC sizes are intuitive: larger mm² = larger wire. Standard IEC sizes: 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2.5, 4, 6, 10, 16, 25, 35, 50, 70, 95, 120, 150, 185, 240, 300 mm².
