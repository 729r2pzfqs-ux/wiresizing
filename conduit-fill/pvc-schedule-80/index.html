About PVC Schedule 80 (Rigid PVC Conduit, Schedule 80)

Heavy-wall PVC required where conduit is exposed to physical damage, such as risers on poles and service masts. Same outside diameter as Schedule 40, so the thicker wall takes noticeably more room from the inside.
