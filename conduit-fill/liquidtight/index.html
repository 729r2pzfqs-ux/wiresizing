About Liquidtight (LFMC) (Liquidtight Flexible Metal Conduit (LFMC))

Flexible metal conduit with a sunlight- and oil-resistant thermoplastic jacket. The usual choice for outdoor equipment whips: air conditioners, pumps, rooftop units.
